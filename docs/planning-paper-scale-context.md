# Paper-qualified planning scales

This page records the earlier scale stage. The later [original text-transform
upgrade](source-text-transforms.md) resolves the Wicker title screen and exposes
a regional recovery budget blocker.

Source-local area layouts can now interpret one or two literal paper-qualified
scale rows below a single visible site/block plan title. Both `1:250 @ A1` and
`1/250 @ A1` notation are supported. Each original text fragment is retained
and screened; no council catalogue title or inferred best-fit size supplies
scale or drawing state.

The eligible rows must share the title's reading direction, lie below it within
six title heights and start within three title heights horizontally. The original
uncropped page must use ordinary PDF units and match exactly one printed ISO
paper size (the existing 1.5 mm dimension tolerance). The alternative rows must
name different papers and agree with ISO square-root-of-two scaling within 1%
for printed rounding. Duplicate, conflicting, unsupported, hidden and missing
claims remain holds. Older paper declarations may bypass the paper-conflict hold
only when every visible declaration binds an exact native scale row in the
validated alternative set. An unrelated paper declaration still withholds the
result. A page-wide scale is never inherited by a floor view or a
mixed-view sheet. The older inline and adjacent-caption paths remain supported.

The saved scale receipt identifies the selected paper and hashes the unchanged
selected row and eligible alternative inventory. Reload validates that receipt,
original page dimensions, shared affine, source polygons and relation graph.
This establishes a nominal paper interpretation, not verified physical scale,
surveyed placement, building heights or traversability.

## Native fragments

`architectural-caption-layout-v5` and `native-caption-fragments-v2` retain exact
native line character coverage, a common original font, reading direction and
baseline. The architectural collector allows at most 5% variation in fragment
size; every non-whitespace fragment still receives its independent original-font
raster check. Whitespace retains its source character/trace records without
claiming visible ink. Overlapping runs in other fonts cannot replace characters
in the line; the combined native text must still equal the original line text
apart from whitespace. No glyph-screen acceptance threshold changed.

Planning job implementation contracts now pin both the fragment collector and
paper-scale resolver. Older v4 standalone area checkpoints with inline/adjacent
scales remain reloadable; old analysis jobs must rerun under the new contract.

## Current real-source limitation

On the previously inspected Wicker woodland-path site plan, the full original
`Site Plan Proposed showing Woodland Path` title is now retained as one seven-
fragment hypothesis. Both original `1/250 @ A1` and `1/500 @ A3` rows pass their
fragment screens. The original page dimensions match A1.

The title remains withheld: its red `showing` and `Path` fragments fail the
existing glyph consistency screen. The paper-selection route therefore does
not produce a real area frame or world on this drawing. The explicit hold is
`complete_plan_caption_glyph_evidence_required`. It would be unsafe to accept
only the black prefix or weaken raster thresholds to force this sheet through.
The next source blocker is faithful rendering/binding of those original coloured
text transforms. Further height/roof and physical registration evidence will
still be needed for reconstructed buildings.

The synthetic desktop check uses A1/A3 alternatives end to end through a saved
flat preview world, native readback and restart. Its two buildings remain
footprint markers. Synthetic success and this development replay establish no
park accuracy, accepted training labels or neural-weight improvement.
