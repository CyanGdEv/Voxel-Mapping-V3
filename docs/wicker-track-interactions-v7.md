# Wicker track passages and nearby timber interpretation

The v7 world fits the three separate track visits through Big Bob and applies
the user's instruction to replace nearby source leaf cells with dark oak
fences. It builds on the v6 sculpture; the older profiles and delivered archives
remain available for comparison.

## Track and structure

The original planning route and retained deck must replay exactly before any
revision is accepted: all 4,692 track cells, materials, station samples and
reserved air match the pinned parent. The source voxel export uses nominal
track Y plus one for its horizontal Z row. Passage projection now follows that
same inverse raster convention. Missing rotated centre columns use neighbouring
samples of the same station branch, never a different track at the same XY.

The explicitly estimated `estimated_turn_crossing` control changes from 190.5
to 189.0 m ODN. All 14 printed HP/LP control values and the horizontal route are
unchanged. Existing terrain-fit offsets remain; preserving printed controls
does not imply that every displayed level equals its printed value. The revised
crossing keeps at least three metres between stacked solid decks and rejects
changes that enter the retained terrain. Lower candidate estimates were rejected
because they reduced this separation.

Existing brown-footprint supports retain their ground positions and assigned
track branch. The revision retargets 130 members, reroutes 11, and withholds
three that cannot fit the revised clearance. It creates no new source footings.
The retained-detail report records the new endpoints and withheld members.

Three station windows define distinct swept sculpture openings. Each reserves
a 3.5 m wide, at least 3 m high model slot above its own deck; overlapping XY
coordinates never collapse the upper and lower visits into one opening. Jambs
and lintels follow the local route normal and grade. Clipped frame fragments
connect to the surviving timber without entering reserved passage cells. One
shoulder emitter is raised onto a clear timber pad above the upper opening.

The native verifier checks actual deck materials and three metres above the
centre path, including slab halves and the 1.5 m collision height of fences.
It also checks saved overhead structure at all six portals. Existing side rails
and barriers may occupy the sides of the wider model slots; they do not excuse
obstructions on the centre path. No banking measurements or operable coaster
simulation are claimed.

## Two-block foliage rule

Source leaf occupancy within two blocks of the actual Wicker Man solid deck is
eligible for dark oak fence interpretation. Distance is Euclidean in three
dimensions, measured between voxel bounds, including half-height slabs. An
upper canopy cell does not qualify solely because it shares a map position with
the ride. Stacked decks use the nearest actual vertical section.

The rule includes source leaf observations previously suppressed by the bounded
structural vegetation mask, recovering potential timber observations as well as
converting visible foliage. It does not invent new source cell positions or
rewrite survey classifications. Native comparison reports distinguish visible
leaf replacements from restored cells that were air in v6.

Ground, buildings, water, existing architecture, paving headroom, riding space
and sculpture openings retain priority. Fences that would enter riding space
are withheld and their source leaf cells are cleared there. The full fence
collision height is checked against ceilings and all native track centre
samples. This policy applies to the retained Wicker Man wooden ride; it does
not turn steel rides or unrelated woodland into wooden fences.

## Evidence and review

Eighty-five tests cover distance boundaries, half slabs, stacked routes, negative
world coordinates, walking and riding headroom, native fence output, separate
vertical passages, connected framing and padded emitters, alongside the earlier
generation checks. The native verifier compares all 9,100 chunks against the
delivered v6 archive. Every changed cell must belong to an explicit track/support
delta, figure/opening proposal or accepted source foliage cell. It checks other
reference roles, water, figure connectivity, open horn curls and campfire block
entities. The evidence JSON records actual counts and checks.

Fixed-camera previews use saved native blocks and external vanilla textures.
They are diagnostic renders, not Minecraft client screenshots. The comparison
uses the user's two previous inspection positions; camera aims and lighting are
approximate. Neither source imagery nor vanilla texture files are redistributed
with the world.

Planning geometry and dated LiDAR constrain this reconstruction. LiDAR height
bands do not identify individual rails or prove the revised estimated crest.
Figure surfaces, hidden framing and the 189.0 m crossing remain source-assisted
modelling decisions. Metre voxels limit small details. Geometry and save/reopen
checks do not certify surveyed accuracy or visual likeness.
