Mapping source comparison

Layers share the aerial image extent. Toggle sources and adjust opacity to compare boundaries. Drag to pan; scroll to zoom.

These are comparison observations. Alignment by coordinates does not establish surveyed accuracy. Missing map features do not establish grass or paving, and agreement between related datasets is not independent verification.

Draft ground colours: blue paving, red structures, green grass, dark blue water; canopy is amber and non-canopy teal. Unknown labels are transparent. LiDAR raster colours run from blue (low) to red (high), with missing values transparent; the valid-data layer shows valid cells green and missing cells grey. Historic England markers show camera locations, not the ground area covered by a photograph. Raw-return markers are a displayed sample of observed points. Historical aerial dates in layer names are publication dates.

How to use this comparison
Start with one vector layer over the reference image. Check stable corners and boundaries across the image before comparing small paths. Use historical imagery to investigate shadows and canopy gaps. Keep proposed plans separate from existing surveys, and account for roof overhangs when comparing roofprints with walls.
Record disagreements and check source dates and lineage. A layer may be empty, unavailable, or awaiting a licensed/local import. Neither a successful request nor a visually close overlay certifies the feature. Notes exported here are review notes only; they do not create training labels or change geometry.
